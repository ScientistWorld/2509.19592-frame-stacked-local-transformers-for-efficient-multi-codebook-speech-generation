# Constraints

Use the fixed dataset generated by `data/generate_synthetic.py` through `scripts/download.sh`.

Do not change the codec dependency rule, train/test split seeds, vocabulary size, sequence length, number of codebooks, or number of speakers when reporting comparable scores.

Do not score wall-clock time. Efficiency comparisons must use `primary_steps_per_24_frames` or another hardware-independent compute proxy.

Do not make the evaluator import `method/` or regenerate method outputs. It must score saved prediction artifacts.
