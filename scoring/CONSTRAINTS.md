# Constraints

- Train on the fixed `data/synthetic_codec/train.pt` and optionally `val.pt`. Do not change the splits, dimensions, speakers or conditioning order. Evaluator fixtures are not training data.
- Produce new utterances learned from these data. Do not call or copy evaluator-side samplers, read test references, copy or splice visible utterances, or repair, filter or construct tokens with an evaluator's validity rule. A hand-written sampler of the data process is not a learned generator, even when its parameters are estimated from the training data.
- Use at most 12 primary sequential steps per 24 frames for 2x and 6 for 4x. The exact artifact declarations are 2 and 4; work within a primary step is unrestricted. The 4x row must come from a generator that runs its primary model at most 6 times per utterance; submitting a 2x (or unstacked) model's samples as the 4x row is a false declaration, even if saved tokens cannot reveal it.
- Submit the raw tensor interface in `briefing/evaluation.md`. No method-written score or verdict is accepted. Contradictory dimensions, impossible values, copied or reused 12-frame halves, distributions no closer than uniform random tokens and failed validity checks fail the run, which then receives the worst value of every scored metric.
- Evaluation freezes comparison outputs, reference outputs, reference samples, operating points, dimensions, thresholds and test size. Reserved baseline outputs and canonical training files cannot be replaced.
- The hidden test never trains a model. It recomputes scientific measurements from raw generated outputs and scores every submitted method through the same interface.
