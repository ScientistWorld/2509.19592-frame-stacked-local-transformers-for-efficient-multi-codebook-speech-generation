# Direction

Develop learned generators that preserve joint codebook structure, temporal coherence, diversity and speaker conditioning while reducing sequential primary generation steps. Improvements to models, local decoding, information passed between steps, training schedules, regularization and sampling are in scope. Model scaling is allowed within the available compute.

The larger-weight 4x operating point studies the high-stacking regime where quality becomes difficult. A solution must improve the actual generated distribution and dependencies, rather than optimize agreement with reference token IDs or exploit reported metadata.

Calling or copying a hidden reference sampler, copying/splicing examples, rule-based validity repair, false compute declarations, modifying canonical data or fixed comparisons, and substituting scores are shortcuts. A learned alternative that produces a better distribution is a scientific improvement, even when its architecture differs from the reference. The separate shortcut review remains necessary for partial copying and unverifiable computation claims.
