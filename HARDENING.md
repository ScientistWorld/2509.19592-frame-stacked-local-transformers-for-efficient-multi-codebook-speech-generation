# Hardening

The original audit found that random tokens could outrank the reproduced method, answers and their generator were public, method-written stack factors bought arbitrary efficiency rewards, and comparison artifacts were editable. The first bundled job introduced temporal structure and full-covariance distribution measurements, but its short-window copy rule rejected stronger learned models and it lacked normalized, feasibility-first ranking.

This final patch batch fixes artifact validation, comparison/data integrity, normalized ranking and the copy rule; withholds executable reference samplers and old answer artifacts under `eval/test/`; and enlarges each evaluation half from 2048 to 8192 utterances. Training tensors and training/model code are unchanged by this final batch. Reference, baseline and variant predictions are regenerated on the final conditioning inputs from their saved trained weights. The submitted job rescales no paper-reported results and performs no evaluator-triggered training.

Verification results, paired uncertainty, before/after shortcut scores, and remaining limitations will be filled from `eval/test/hardening/summary.json` when the single bundled verification job completes.
